Approach

How I Work

Research

I run interviews, thematic analysis, and design ethnography, plus structured observation and behavioral measures. I use them to find out what people actually need and what the design problem really is.

Accessibility

I check work against WCAG 2.2, but compliance is the bare minimum. I design for edge cases, because a page can pass every check and still be hard to use.

Design

I turn research findings into interfaces and interaction patterns. I care about usability and about visual design that's genuinely beautiful.

Collaboration

Most of my work has been on teams, and I keep things organized: clear files, decisions written down, documentation that stays current.
