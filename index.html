Hi, I'm Erin

I'm based in the Bay Area with a Master's in Human-Computer Interaction and a background in cognitive science and psychology.

Technology is everywhere and getting more capable fast, and I find that genuinely exciting. It's also why I think design matters: making things worth people's time and trust, understanding what they actually need, keeping things from going sideways. Those are the problems that motivate me.
