How I work

I study what people actually do with technology.

Research

I choose research methods based on the context and the question at hand, then apply them: interviews, thematic analysis, design ethnography, structured observation, or behavioral measures. The goal is to find out what people actually need and what the design problem is.

Design

I turn research findings into interfaces and interaction patterns. I care about usability and about visual design that’s beautiful.

Accessibility

I check work against WCAG 2.2, but compliance is the bare minimum. I design for edge cases, because a page can pass every check and still be hard to use.

Documentation

I keep my work well organized so it’s useful at handoff: clear files, decisions written down, documentation that stays current.
